Each card shows the actual frames identification read (click the image to flip cover ⇄ front-matter) beside what was deciphered. ✓ Correct records your confirmation. ✗ Wrong fails closed: within ~2 minutes the local agent routes that video back to the scan pipeline and quarantines any download. Verdicts persist on the Mac; “applied ✓” means the local side has acted. The “match” number on downloaded books is text-overlap between pages OCR’d from the middle of your video and the downloaded file — right books measure 40–90% (OCR noise eats the rest), wrong books <2%. It is a fingerprint comparison, not a model’s opinion.
